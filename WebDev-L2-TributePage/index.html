A life dedicated to science and learning

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Scientist, teacher, and the People's President who inspired a generation to dream bigger.

His Story

Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, a small island town in Tamil Nadu. When he was about ten, his science teacher at the local school drew a bird on the blackboard and explained how it flies. Many students said they had not understood, so the teacher took the whole class to the seashore that evening and let them watch real birds in flight. Kalam later described that evening as the moment that pointed his life towards flight and science.

That curiosity took him to the Madras Institute of Technology, where he studied aeronautical engineering. He later joined ISRO and served as Project Director of SLV-III, India's first indigenous satellite launch vehicle. In July 1980 it placed the Rohini satellite in orbit.

He then moved to the Defence Research and Development Organisation and headed the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme, which produced missiles such as Agni and Prithvi. From 1992 to 1999 he was Scientific Adviser to the Defence Minister and Secretary of the Department of Defence Research and Development.

He became the 11th President of India in 2002 and served until 2007, yet he kept making time for school students across the country. What I admire most is how simple his lesson was: one good teacher, one evening by the sea, and a ten-year-old found his life's work. It reminds me to ask questions when I do not understand something, and to keep learning by seeing things with my own eyes.

Key Moments

  1. 1931 Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.
  2. 1980 As Project Director, saw SLV-III place the Rohini satellite in orbit.
  3. 1981 Awarded the Padma Bhushan.
  4. 1990 Awarded the Padma Vibhushan.
  5. 1992–1999 Served as Scientific Adviser to the Defence Minister.
  6. 1997 Awarded the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian honour.
  7. 2002 Became the 11th President of India and served until 2007.

Words to Remember

“Dream, dream and dream. Dream transforms into thoughts. Thoughts result into action.”

Message shared by Dr. Kalam in his address at City High School, Berhampur, 4 July 2006
